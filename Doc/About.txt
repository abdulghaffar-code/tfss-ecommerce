Our Company


Total Fire Safety Company was established in 1992 by Mr. Muhammad Rizwan Siddique, whose respected late father Haji Muhammad Siddique was the founder of Pakistan’s first Safety Wears’ Manufacturing Setup, Pak Gloves Manufacturing Company, which was established in 1969 and also keeps the honor of being the pioneer of self-manufactured safety wears & other safety-related PPE (Personal Protective Equipment).

In 1992, Mr. Muhammad Rizwan Siddique S/o. Mr. Muhammad Siddique took charge of this company and started looking after the company matters, utilizing his experience and knowledge that is a unique specialty of this company. He started to further extend the involvement of the next generations in the same field under the supervision of his father.After holding the charge, Mr. Muhammad Rizwan Siddique extended the circle of his company’s trade and added almost every item along with self-manufactured safety wears that is required at an engineering and standard hardware setup. This was for the convenience of standard and engineering workshops, as per the demanded quality products. The brought-in articles turned into a larger circle of trade, and the business gradually stepped into a larger range and became “General Order Suppliers” in a larger range.
